what a later separately reviewed minimal design would need; they do not confer

## Bounded ownership and evidence

- One immutable object/subscription identity and one-shot ownership lifecycle per
  selected role; no unbounded re-subscription/reconciliation supervisor. FD
  aliases/inheritance, daemon replacement, disabled tracepoints, lost producer
  scope or unknown close/disposition are epoch failures, not recovery actions.
- Timerfd cancellation read is destructive. Its ECANCELED must be latched before
  any possible next action; subsequent EAGAIN/expiry cannot erase it. No second
  reader, settime, restoring ioctl, disarm, recreated timer or retry can revalidate
  an old attempt. A setup error can have changed state; never assume "not set".
- For any proposed event transport, fixed schema/count/buffer limits, authentic
  source and subscription IDs, scope, source-controlled enablement, producer/
  consumer ordering, loss counters and covered frontier must be evidenced.
  Unknown pending/trailing loss, overflow, counters wrapping, missing CPU stream,
  control-state change or diagnostic failure remains UNKNOWN/HOLD. No increasing
  ring/ledger ceiling, ignored event payload or reset to make the stream fit.
- C1 historical reads remain exact in-place read-only locked originals; no
  copied markers, clearing/repair/lock reacquisition or first-pin-on-failure.
  Failed exits independently attempt already-owned categories even if the private
  ledger is unsafe. Preserve first failure and separate secondary outcomes.
  Short/unknown write or fsync poisons all further appends, including diagnostics;
  no write/fsync retry. Clear owned host FD slot before one close, no numeric-FD
  retry or assertion that a failed close retained it. No target ownership cleanup.
- Event/raw clocks/host source/privilege/namespace/service/provenance evidence
  stays bounded and private outside Git. Public report only fixed outcomes and
  public source pins; no private paths/digests/UUIDs/absolute clock values/raw
  events/credentials. A source checksum/dataclass/digest is not proof it ran.

## Minimum unresolved host facts / privileges (NOT queried or requested now)

1. Actual executing kernel and userspace source/build/configuration association,
   relevant syscall/PM/tracing support and clock-domain/time-namespace mappings;
   source declarations or camera6.18.55 are not evidence of actual host behavior.
2. Who can set/step time or initiate each form of sleep, including direct/alternate
   paths, bypass grants, privileged management, kernel/firmware and virtualization;
   which enforced trust boundary prevents them through the complete interval.
3. Exact logind/service policy/source and original object lifetime if cooperative
   inhibition is used; no pending sleep, restarts or bypass during coverage.
   Merely having the right to acquire an inhibitor is insufficient. ALARM wake
   capability or perf privileges do not grant the missing exclusion guarantee.
4. For event detection, actual source-backed activation and all-producer scope,
   privilege/access policy, loss/frontier semantics and ownership exclusions.
   Notification permission or zero sampled losses alone cannot prove these.
5. Applicable PID starttime conversion/rounding/namespace basis and authentic
   original recorder native deadline/peer/start/source/catch/RXTX lineage. These
   remain separate C2c dependencies, not supplied by this lifecycle table.
